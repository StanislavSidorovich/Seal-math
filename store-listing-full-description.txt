Help Sausage the Seal rescue friends across the Arctic — and practice real math along the way!

A storm has scattered Sausage's friends across the icy ocean. Sail from island to island, solve friendly math problems to clear the path, and reunite with penguins, whales, and other Arctic animals stranded along the way.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 8 themed islands, each with its own math focus and story
• Math topics that grow with your child: addition and subtraction, multiplication and division, fractions, word problems, patterns, and more — the game adapts to the level your child is actually at
• Short bonus mini-games between missions (catch fish, find hidden penguins, and more) to keep things playful
• A customizable seal character — unlock costumes, accessories, and pets as rewards for progress
• A daily rescue mission and a friendly streak counter to build a small, positive habit
• A simple parent dashboard showing time played and accuracy by topic
• Full English and Russian language support, with an optional "learning mode" that shows both languages side by side

BUILT FOR FAMILIES, NOT FOR ENGAGEMENT METRICS
• No ads, ever
• No account or sign-in required
• No internet connection needed to play
• No data collection of any kind — everything stays on your child's device
• A quick parental gate (a simple math problem) keeps the parent dashboard and reset tools out of curious little hands

Designed for children roughly ages 6–11, Arctic Math Rescue aims to make math practice feel like an adventure with a friend, not a worksheet — one rescued animal, one solved problem at a time.
