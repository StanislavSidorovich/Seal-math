Privacy Policy for Arctic Math Rescue

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Arctic Math Rescue ("the app") is an offline educational math game for children. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to information when you use the app.

Summary

The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, to us or to anyone else. There is no internet connection required or used by the app at any point during gameplay.

What information is stored, and where

When you create a player profile (a name and an emoji you choose) and play through the game, that information — along with progress, scores, and settings — is saved only on your own device, using standard local device storage. This data:

  • never leaves your device
  • is never transmitted to us, to Google, or to any third party
  • is not linked to any online account, email address, or identity
  • is only ever readable by the app itself, on the same device

No accounts, no ads, no third parties

The app does not require creating an account or signing in. It contains no advertising, no analytics software, no crash-reporting services, and no third-party SDKs of any kind. It makes no network requests — everything needed to play is bundled with the app itself.

Children's privacy

Arctic Math Rescue is designed for children roughly ages 6–11 and is built to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, including children, there is nothing collected that would require parental consent, and nothing for us to disclose, sell, or share.

Deleting your data

Since all data lives only on your device, you are always in full control of it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app's storage from your device's settings, permanently deletes every player profile and all progress. There is no server-side copy anywhere for us to delete, because none is ever created.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this same page with a new "Last updated" date above.
